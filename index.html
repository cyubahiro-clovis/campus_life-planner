About

Why I made this

My week was split between a calendar app, group chats and sticky notes. I wanted one place that shows what's due, how much time I've signed up for, and when I'm taking on too much.

It's plain HTML, CSS, and Javascript with no frameworks. Your tasks are saved in this browser with localStorage, so there's no account and nothing is sent anywhere.
